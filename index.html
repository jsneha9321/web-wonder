FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes 1–2 weeks once we have your content. Online stores take 2–4 weeks.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. We build on WordPress and show you how to update text, images and products.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

We can help you choose and set up hosting and a domain. You always own them.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer a monthly maintenance plan, and small fixes right after launch are free.
