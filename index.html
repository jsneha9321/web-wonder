From the blog

A few things worth knowing before you build.

Basics

10 Reasons Every Small Business Needs a Website

Social media pages get you discovered, but they don't build trust the way an owned website does. A website is where people check you're real before they call, compare your prices without messaging you first, and find you on Google when they're not already following you — the four things a business genuinely needs to grow beyond word of mouth.

Platform

WordPress vs Webflow: Which One Fits Your Business?

WordPress wins when you want full ownership, thousands of plugins, and the ability to hand content edits to anyone on your team. Webflow wins when design control matters more than plugin flexibility and you're comfortable with a steeper learning curve. For most small businesses that plan to update content often, WordPress is still the safer long-term bet.

Conversion

Common Website Mistakes That Cost You Leads

The three that hurt most: a contact form buried below three scrolls, a homepage that explains features before it explains who the business actually helps, and a site that takes over five seconds to load on mobile. Fixing just these three, before touching anything else, is usually where the biggest conversion gains come from.
