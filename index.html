FAQ

Questions before you book.

How long does a website take?

Starter takes 7–10 days and Business 2–3 weeks from when we receive your content and logo. Premium depends on scope.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (or we can design one), page text, photos, and domain/hosting details. We'll guide you on content if you're not sure what to write.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. WordPress sites come with a short handover so you can update text, images and blog posts without a developer.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. They're billed at cost and the accounts stay in your name.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — 15 days of free fixes after launch, then maintenance from ₹999/month.

Can you fix or redesign my existing site?

Yes. Send the link and we'll review speed, SEO and design before quoting.

Do you design creatives and post on social media?

Yes. We design posters, banners, flyers and social media posts, and we can handle daily posting on your Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn pages. The price depends on how many posts you need each month, so ask for a custom quote.

Project terms

  • Payment: 50% advance, 50% before launch.
  • Revisions: 2 rounds on design, 2 on the built site.
  • Ownership: you own the site and accounts after final payment.
  • Timeline: starts once content and advance are received.
  • Scope: extra work is quoted and approved first.
